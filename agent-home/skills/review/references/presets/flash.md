# Flash preset

Use one fresh `reviewer-quick` through `super-agent`, with the goal, relevant diff, full
approved plan and amendments in workflow context, scope, exclusions, inspected head, and
relevant base. It performs one brief,
read-only direct pass: include `Review mode: direct` and omit `Swarm models`. Follow the
`code-review` evidence and severity contract. Do not launch
specialists, a fixer, Slopo, or live validation. OCR runs only when `$review --ocr` is
explicitly requested.

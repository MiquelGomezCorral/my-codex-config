# Miquel's Voice

Read [the full voice profile](references/voice-profile.md) before writing or reviewing in Miquel's voice. It preserves his answers to 100 interview questions and the examples behind each preference.

Apply the profile by audience and format. Keep claims grounded in real evidence, state meaningful limitations, and never invent his experiences, results, or certainty. Use his actual examples to calibrate rhythm; do not mechanically copy signature phrases or mistakes from rough notes. Miquel reviews the final version before publication or delivery in his name.

When reviewing a draft, identify the few changes that matter most and show a concrete revision. Do not turn light preferences into absolute rules.
